What is HELION?

HELION is a 3-day student-hosted technology and innovation festival in Bengaluru, bringing together students to compete, build, experiment and showcase what they can create. From hackathons and cybersecurity to robotics, game development, engineering, coding, design and ideation, HELION brings multiple disciplines together under one festival, built for students ready to create beyond the expected.
